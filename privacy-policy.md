# Privacy Policy

Sick Log doesn't collect any data. There's no server, no account, no analytics,
no ads, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

**Your data:** everything you enter is stored on your device and, if you're
signed in to iCloud, in your own private iCloud account through Apple. The
developer can't see or access it. You can turn off iCloud for the app in iOS
Settings.

**Sharing:** your data only leaves your device when you choose to send it — for
example, by exporting a file or sharing with someone. It goes only where you
send it.

**Children:** the app is often used to record information about children. Since
the developer never receives any data, that information stays with you.

**Purchases:** handled by Apple. The app never sees your payment details.

**Deleting your data:** delete anything in the app at any time. Deleting the app
removes its data from your device, and you can remove the iCloud copy in iOS
Settings → your name → iCloud.
