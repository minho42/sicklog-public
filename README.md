# Sick Log

An iPhone app for keeping track of when you, your kids, or anyone you care for
is sick — what it was, how long it lasted, and how they were along the way.

## What you can record

- **Sick logs** — one for each illness: who was sick, with what, and for how
  long, with the symptoms and notes for each day as it goes on.
- **Temperatures** — readings with their times, with fevers flagged.
- **Medications** — what was given, how much, and when.
- **Visits** — doctor's appointments, tests, and vaccinations, past or
  upcoming, sick or not.

Temperatures, medications, and visits belong to the person, so they don't need
a sick log of their own, and those during an illness show up in its sick log.

## Looking back

- **Calendar** — a month at a glance, with each person in their own colour.
- **Logs** — every sick log in one list, with everything recorded during it.
- **People** — each person's sick logs and their full history.
- **Export** — save everything as a CSV or JSON file whenever you like.

## iCloud and sharing

If you're signed in to iCloud, your data is saved to your own private iCloud
account — on the free version too, not just Pro. It syncs across your devices
and comes back if you reinstall the app or move to a new iPhone.

Sharing logs with other people — a partner, grandparent, or carer — isn't
available yet. It may come in a future update if there's demand for it. If
you'd like it, get in touch using the support address below.

## Privacy

Your data stays on your device and in your own iCloud account. There's no
server, no account to create, no analytics, and no ads. See the
[privacy policy](privacy-policy.md) for details.

## Pricing

Sick Log is free for one person, with every feature included. A one-time
in-app purchase, Sick Log Pro, lets you add more than one person.

## Not medical advice

Sick Log is a record-keeping tool, not a medical device. It doesn't diagnose,
treat, or give advice — including about medicines or doses. If you're worried
about anyone's health, talk to a doctor or pharmacist.
